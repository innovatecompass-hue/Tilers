How Much Does Bathroom Tile Installation Cost in Shreveport, LA?

If you're planning a bathroom renovation in Shreveport or Bossier City, tile installation is likely your biggest line item. Here's a straightforward breakdown of what to expect — from a licensed local contractor who gives free quotes with no surprises.

Project Type Typical Range (Installed)
Shower surround (ceramic/porcelain) $1,200 – $3,500
Shower surround (natural stone/large format) $2,500 – $6,000+

These are installed prices — materials plus labor. Exact costs depend on the factors below.

What Affects the Final Price?

1. Tile Material

The tile itself is usually 40–60% of the total project cost. Here's a rough material-only breakdown per square foot:

  • Basic ceramic: $1–$4/sqft
  • Porcelain (standard): $3–$8/sqft
  • Large format porcelain: $5–$15/sqft
  • Natural stone (marble, travertine): $8–$25/sqft
  • Glass or mosaic: $10–$30/sqft

2. Labor

In Shreveport, expect to pay $6–$12 per square foot for installation labor, depending on tile size, pattern complexity, and access. Intricate patterns like herringbone or diagonal layouts take longer and cost more.

3. Surface Prep

If the existing substrate needs waterproofing, cement board, or leveling work, add $200–$800 to the project. Skipping proper prep is the #1 cause of tile failure — don't let any contractor skip it.

4. Demo & Removal

Removing old tile typically adds $200–$600 depending on square footage and what's underneath. Tear-out of fiberglass surround inserts can add complexity.

5. Extras

Niches, built-in shelves, heated floor prep, and custom trim work all add to the total. We itemize every line on our quotes so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Is Tile Installation More Expensive in Shreveport Than the National Average?

Generally, no. Shreveport and Bossier City tend to run 10–20% below national averages for home improvement labor costs. That said, material prices have risen like everywhere else since 2022. Getting 2–3 local quotes is always smart.
